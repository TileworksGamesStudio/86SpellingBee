RULES & GUIDE

How to Play

Core Objective

Construct as many words as possible using the 7 letters shown in the honeycomb. Always include the center letter in every single word.

Rules of the Hive

  • Words must be at least 4 letters long.
  • Every word must contain the center letter at least once.
  • Letters can be reused multiple times.
  • Proper nouns, slang, obscenities, and hyphenated words are excluded.

Point System

1 pt 4-letter words
1 pt / letter Words with 5+ letters
+7 pts bonus Pangram (uses all 7 letters)
👑 Queen Bee Discover 100% of all puzzle words!

Tips & Shortcuts

Use your keyboard directly! Letters input words, Space or / shuffles the outer hive, Backspace deletes, and Enter submits.
